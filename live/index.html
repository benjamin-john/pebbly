    Your quizzes

      How it works

      • Make a quiz: questions with 2 to 6 answers. Mark the right one, or leave it as a poll.
      • Tap Present on a laptop connected to a projector or TV. A QR code and a short code let everyone join from their phone.
      • Show each question, watch the answers come in, then reveal the answer and how everyone voted.
      • Turn on names to see everyone’s scores at the end and download them.
      • Made it on your phone? Tap 🔗 Open on another device to show it on a laptop or projector (a display screen with no controls) and keep the controls on your phone.
