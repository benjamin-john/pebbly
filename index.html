Pebbly

Small, free tools for planning get-togethers and playing games.

Plan

  • ✉️ RSVP
    Send an invite, collect replies, and see who's coming and what they're bringing.
  • 📅 Which Day Works?
    Share a few dates and let everyone mark when they're free.
  • 🗳️ Poll
    Ask a question, list the choices, and let the group vote.

Play

  • 🎯 Darts Scorer
    Score 301, 501 and Cricket dart by dart, with busts, checkouts and winners handled for you.
  • 🎲 Farkle
    Dice roller and scoreboard for any number of players, with an optional “continue play” mode.
  • 🎲 Qwixx
    Dice roller and score cards for any number of players. Use either one or both.
  • 🧮 Score Keeper
    Keep score for any game: a column per player, round by round, with running totals.
  • 🧠 The Name Game
    A party memory game: everyone adds a name, then the group tries to recall the whole list.
