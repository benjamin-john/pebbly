Pebbly

Small, free tools for planning get-togethers and playing games.

Plan

  • ✉️ RSVP
    Send an invite, collect replies, and see who's coming and what they're bringing.
  • 📅 Which Day Works?
    Share a few dates and let everyone mark when they're free.
  • 🗳️ Poll
    Ask a question, list the choices, and let the group vote.
  • 📋 Sign-up Sheet
    Who's bringing what and helping when: people put their name down from the link.
  • 👶 Baby Names
    Swipe through names on your own phones and see the ones you both love, in your own order.
  • 💸 Split the Bill
    Track who paid what on a trip or night out, and see the fewest payments to settle up.
  • 🛒 Shared List
    One list everyone edits together, for groceries, packing or to-dos, ticked off live.
  • 🎁 Secret Santa
    Draw names for a gift swap. Everyone sees only who they're buying for.

Play

  • 🏆 Bracket
    Run a knockout tournament: tap winners, see the whole bracket, crown a champion.
  • 🔐 Codebreaker
    Hide a secret color code and race to crack each other's. Play a friend or the computer.
  • 🎨 Color Stack
    A sorting puzzle: move beads one at a time until every rod holds just one color.
  • 🎯 Darts Scorer
    Score 301, 501 and Cricket dart by dart, with busts, checkouts and winners handled for you.
  • ✏️ Dots and Boxes
    Draw lines, close boxes, go again. 2 to 4 players, or play the computer.
  • 🎲 Farkle
    Dice roller and scoreboard for any number of players, with an optional “continue play” mode.
  • ⏱️ Game Timer
    A chess-style turn clock for board games, or a countdown for charades.
  • ⚫ Gomoku
    Five in a row on a Go board. Play a friend on one phone or two, or the computer.
  • 🎲 Kwiks
    Dice roller and score cards for any number of players. Use either one or both.
  • 📊 Live Quiz
    Quiz or poll a room: questions on the big screen, everyone answers on their phone.
  • 🫘 Mancala
    The classic seed-sowing game: capture seeds and fill your store. Play a friend or the computer.
  • 🧠 The Name Game
    A party memory game: everyone adds a name, then the group tries to recall the whole list.
  • 🧮 Score Keeper
    Keep score for any game: a column per player, round by round, with running totals.
  • 🎲 Team Picker
    Split into fair teams, pick someone at random, or shuffle the turn order.
  • ❓ Trivia
    A family quiz. Make your own questions or have AI write them; play on one phone or everyone's.
  • 🔤 Word Dash
    Guess the five-letter word in six tries. Race friends on their phones, or practice on your own.
