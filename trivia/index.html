Trivia

A quiz for the family. Read it out on one phone, or everyone answers on their own phone.

Question sets

    How it works

    • Pick a question set, or make your own. Import / AI gives you a prompt to paste into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, which writes a whole quiz on any topic.
    • This phone: the quizmaster reads each question, reveals the answer and taps who got it right.
    • Everyone’s phones: others join with a code and tap their answers. This phone runs the quiz.
    • One point for each right answer. Most points wins.
